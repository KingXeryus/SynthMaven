# SynthMaven 2.1.1

Hold **ENV for three seconds** to open **CHORDS**. The first three assignable knobs edit chord mode, voicing and strum. The screen stays open after release. A short ENV press opens the envelope pages; HOME returns to the home screen. No chord shortcut screen appears before the full three-second hold.

This patch retains the 2.1 sounds, tuning, graphics, other shortcuts and protocol 22. See [2.1 features and limitations](release-2.1.md).

## Installation and recovery

Back up programs and settings before installing. Use the [installer](https://kingxeryus.github.io/SynthMaven/webapp/installer/) in Chrome or Edge, connect the FM-1 by USB, and keep power connected during installation. Downloadable firmware and SHA256 checksums accompany this release. Follow the [quick-start and recovery instructions](../QUICKSTART.md) and [manual](USER_MANUAL.md). Keep the original firmware available for recovery.

## Known limitations

Physical FM-1 hardware validation remains pending. Eight voices are shared across the four parts. Direct USB pedals require a powered adapter or hub and hardware validation; Bluetooth is unavailable. Vital imports are native HYBRID approximations; USER WT is intentionally silent until populated. AUTO/AUTO2 bypass applicable master microtuning as in 2.1.
