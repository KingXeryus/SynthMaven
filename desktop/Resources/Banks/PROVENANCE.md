# SynthMaven software library 0.2

- 449 existing firmware presets, retaining existing provenance in LICENSING.md.
- 2694 original performance variations: six named articulations per existing
  preset. Source and recipe are recorded in each FactorySound. These are
  variations, not 2694 unrelated imported patches.
- 208 original modeled Oriental / Middle Eastern instrument articulations:
  52 parameter recipes, four performance treatments each. These approximate
  timbral families in SynthMaven engines. No proprietary samples are included.
- 32 YSE original FM voices, licensed CC0-1.0 by their author. Bank downloaded
  from https://github.com/yvanvds/yse-soundengine/tree/dev/content/fm/original
  on 2026-10-25. The upstream CONTENT-LICENSES.md is included beside the bank.

YSE source license:
https://github.com/yvanvds/yse-soundengine/blob/dev/CONTENT-LICENSES.md

Installed Yamaha ESP and TAQS.IM SOLO were inspected for available formats
and tonal reference. Two local SOLO presets supplied general filter/envelope
and panning references for original modeled voices. Their preset binaries,
waveforms, commercial sample libraries and account data are not distributed.
Neither product's proprietary sound engine is included or directly imported.
